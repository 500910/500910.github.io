01 / THE MAKER

A bow is not
an accessory.

It is the connection between hand and sound.

ArchetBow is a dedicated workshop centered on the craft of the violin bow. Each bow begins with the character of its wood and ends with the character of its player.

Under the direction of Mengqing Chen, the workshop pursues balance, responsiveness and clarity while respecting the traditions of fine bow making.

04 / THE WORKSHOP

Tradition,
reconsidered.

Every detail matters: the selection of wood, the geometry of the stick, the camber, the frog, the weight and — above all — how the bow answers the player's hand.

ArchetBow brings that patient, exacting approach to a new generation of musicians and collectors.
